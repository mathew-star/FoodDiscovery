      Ask Hyderabad
      ▲318 Do I need an account to use FDN? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      No. Search, dish rankings, restaurant pages, posts and lists are all open without signing in, forever. You can even save up to 20 places on your phone. You only sign in, with Google or your phone number, when you want to rate, post, comment or vote.

      ▲276 Why Loved, Good, Okay and Skip instead of stars? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      Because you rate a dish, not a restaurant, and four plain words are faster and more honest than guessing between 3 and 4 stars. A restaurant’s place in rankings comes from how its best dishes are rated, plus quick notes on value, service and ambience.

      ▲241 How do you stop fake ratings and restaurant spam? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      New accounts count for half until they’ve contributed for a few days. Sudden rating bursts, clusters of new accounts and many accounts from one device are held automatically. Every post is checked before it goes live, and moderators review reports, usually within a day.

      ▲198 Can a restaurant pay to rank higher? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      No, and there are no ads. Creators who got a free or paid meal must label the post; those ratings are shown but never counted. Staff and owners who declare where they work have their ratings for that brand set to zero.

      ▲164 What’s a Dish Battle? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      Two places, one dish, a 3 to 7 day public vote. You see the split only after you vote, so early results don’t sway you. Battles are for fun and never change dish scores.

      ▲129 Do you track or store my location? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      We only ask when you choose “show food near you”, and you can pick a locality instead. When you post from a restaurant, your location is checked once to add the “posted on-site” mark, then discarded. We never store your coordinates.

      ▲97 A price or timing is wrong. How do I fix it? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      Tap “Still true?” next to it and choose No, then enter the new value. When enough locals agree, it updates on its own. “Permanently closed” is always checked by our team first.

      ▲71 Can I delete my account and my data? +
      ✓ Answered by FDN

      Yes, from Settings. You can download everything you’ve posted, and deleting your account removes your personal data within 30 days. Your posts stay readable as “deleted user” so threads still make sense.
